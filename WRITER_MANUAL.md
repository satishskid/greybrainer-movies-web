### Step 0 & 1: Discovery & Film Intelligence Intake
*Identify the subject film and pull baseline diagnostic telemetry.*

- **How to do it**:
  1. Go to **Review Inbox** (`/studio/inbox`) to pick an incoming trending title, OR go to the **Movie Engine** (`/` or `/studio/manual`) and type the film's title and release year.
  2. Click **Search / Fetch TMDB**. The system automatically pulls verified metadata: director, primary cast, plot overview, studio credits, and high-resolution posters.
  3. Run the **Three-Layer Analysis Engine**. The AI computes initial metrics across Story (Layer 1), Concept (Layer 2), and Performance (Layer 3).

| ✅ DO | ❌ DON'T |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DO** verify the release year and language match the film you intend to analyze. | **DON'T** analyze films without verified TMDB credits; missing credits break the visual cards. |
| **DO** check the auto-suggested director and cast for accuracy before moving forward. | **DON'T** skip checking the synopsis—ensure it reflects the actual release, not an unrelated remake. |

---

### Step 2: Synthesis & Forensic Dossier Polish
*Craft the comprehensive 7-layer critique, 50-word verdict, and pacing insights.*

- **How to do it**:
  1. Review the generated **50-Word Executive Verdict**. This is the first thing studio executives read—ensure it is punchy, incisive, and objective.
  2. Examine the **Three-Layer Scores** (Story/Script, Conceptualization, Execution/Performance). You have full editorial freedom to adjust the scores if your critical assessment differs from the initial model.
  3. Inspect the **Morphokinetics™ Pacing Telemetry**. Note where narrative tension peaks, plateaus, or lags.
  4. Polish the long-form analysis. Add human nuance, specific scene observations, thematic resonance, and craft evaluations.
  5. The system continuously auto-buffers your changes locally and syncs to sovereign Cloudflare D1/R2 storage.

| ✅ DO | ❌ DON'T |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DO** ensure the critique is deep and comprehensive (aim for 2,000+ words/characters of substantive analysis). | **DON'T** publish shallow 1-paragraph stubs. Institutional readers expect forensic rigor. |
| **DO** include concrete observations about script turning points, character motivations, and directing choices. | **DON'T** use filler phrases like *"This is a good movie that everyone should watch."* Use diagnostic craft terms. |
| **DO** click **"Save Pack"** whenever you finish a major round of writing to create an immutable version (`v1`, `v2`, etc.). | **DON'T** worry about losing work on browser refresh—your draft is backed up locally and in Cloudflare R2. |

### Step 3: Asset Studio & Craftmatics Approval
*Prepare cinematic visual cards and approve craft benchmarks.*

- **How to do it**:
  1. In the Studio, select the **"🎨 1. Production (Asset Studio)"** tab.
  2. **Select Cover & Artwork**: Pick a high-resolution cinematic landscape still for the hero banner, plus vertical posters for social cards. Avoid low-resolution or watermarked images.
  3. **Visual Ratios & Concentric Rings**: Confirm that the concentric 3-layer diagnostic rings render cleanly for Story, Concept, and Performance.
  4. **The Craftmatics Approval Gate**:
     - Review each generated craft criterion (e.g. *Story Architecture*, *Thematic Originality*, *Pacing Velocity*, *Ensemble Resonance*).
     - Click **"Approve this assessment"** for each item.
     - Add a brief 1-line approval rationale (e.g., *"Accurately reflects the structural lag in Act 2"*).
     - *Note: Live publishing is locked until all craft assessments receive human editorial approval.*

| ✅ DO | ❌ DON'T |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DO** choose high-contrast, atmosphere-rich stills that look stunning on desktop and mobile. | **DON'T** use generic empty-theater stock photos when authentic film stills are available in the picker. |
| **DO** approve all Craftmatics criteria; this is our editorial quality seal that unlocks publication. | **DON'T** leave the approval rationale blank—provide a quick justification for the benchmark score. |

## 🛡️ Never Lose Work: The Local + Cloud Resilience System

We know how frustrating it is to lose an hour of deep analytical writing due to an internet glitch or browser freeze. Greybrainer Studio features a **Dual-Layer Fail-Safe**:

1. **Instant Local Auto-Buffer**:
   - Every keystroke and slider adjustment is cached locally in your browser's encrypted storage (`IndexedDB` / `LocalStorage`).
   - If your WiFi drops, your laptop battery dies, or you accidentally close the tab, simply reopen the Studio: **your draft will automatically restore exactly where you left off**.
2. **Immutable Cloudflare R2 Cloud History**:
   - Every time you click **"Save Pack"**, a new version snapshot (`v1`, `v2`, `v3`...) is created on our Cloudflare R2 sovereign storage.
   - You can view version history and compare changes at any time.
